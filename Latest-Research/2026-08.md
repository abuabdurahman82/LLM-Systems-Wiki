## Why it matters
- **Speed as a product dimension** (GPT-5.6 Sol 14×, effort tiers) — test-time compute
  is now a SKU with a price point.
- **Open multimodal returns** (Muse Glimmer) — local agentic + Apache-2.0 lowers the
  cost of the agent-era stack for the open ecosystem.
- **KV research is the hottest serving area** (7 of the 12 tracked preprints are
  KV-focused) — consistent with KV being the decode-era bottleneck
  (`Inference/Roofline.md`).
- **MoE serving systems** matured from "does it fit" to "how do we place experts /
  schedule batches / use new memory tiers."

## What previous assumptions became weaker
- "Decode is just bandwidth" — HBF/near-memory preprints attack the memory hierarchy
  itself (★ experimental).
- "Fixed heuristics are enough for KV eviction" — 4 of 7 KV preprints target learned /
  lifecycle-aware budgets.
- "Reproducibility is a human-review problem" — agent-scale reproduction (and
  agent-scale falsification) changes the loop.

## Which developments are mostly marketing
- "14× speed" (GPT-5.6 Sol): vendor claim, no independent reproduction yet — treat as
  marketing until benchmarked. ★ marketing-flag.
- Vendor benchmark deltas (Frontier-Bench/GDPval-AA claims): unverified.

## Which research deserves deeper attention
1. **KV eviction/compression 2026 wave** — track SPECTRA/KVDiagnosis for a first
   independent quality evaluation.
2. **DeaMoE + systems-aware MoE scaling** — if small-batch MoE decode improves, MoE
   economics shift toward interactive use.
3. **OpScale** — operator-level autoscaling could cut serving waste at the kernel level.
4. **CoRun** — determinism matters for regulated inference (audit/replay).
