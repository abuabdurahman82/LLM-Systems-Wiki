## 30-Second Explanation
The *six* *flagships* (NVIDIA, AMD, TPU, Cerebras, Groq, Trainium) *are the* *deployed* *center of gravity. *Around* *them* *is a* *long tail: (a)* *the* *form-factor* *players* (NPU, DPU, *edge ASIC, *FPGA), *(b)* *the* *reconfigurable* *and* *in-memory* *bets* (reconfigurable dataflow, *compute-in-memory, *analog, *neuromorphic, *photonic), *and* *(c)* *the* *deployed* *non-flagship* *accelerators* (Tenstorrent, *Tesla Dojo/AI5, *Meta MTIA, *Intel Gaudi, *Etched, *MatX, *Taalas). *This* *page* *maps* *the* *tail: for* *each* *direction, *the* *bet, *the* *state* *(announced vs deployed), *and the* *first-principles* *question it* *poses* *to the* *six flagships. *The* *rule:* **the* *flagships* *win* *the* *rack, *but the* *tail* *competes* *for the* *device* *(NPU), the* *data-center* *periphery* (DPU), *and the* *next* *process node's* *dataflow* *(reconfigurable* *is the* *Groq/TSP* *line* *extended to a* *FPGA-like* *substrate) [I].

## The form-factor players (NPU, DPU, edge, FPGA)
| Player | Form factor | The bet | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| NPU (Apple M/S, Snapdragon X, AMD XDNA) | SoC (in-phone/PC) | the *on-device* inference (the *LLM* on the *phone/PC, *page 01's "specialized" bet at the *edge) | *deployed* (Apple *Neural Engine, *Snapdragon *Hexagon, *AMD XDNA* in *Ryzen AI) [F: vendor docs, secondary] |
| DPU (NVIDIA BlueField, Intel IPU/E2000, AMD Pensando) | the *data-center* periphery (the *smartNIC) | offload the *network/storage/security* from the *CPU; the *DPU* *is the* *"third processor"* (after CPU+GPU) [F: vendors] | *deployed* [F: vendors] |
| Edge ASIC (custom inference SoCs) | the *device* | the *$/* *and the* *watt* *are the* *spec; the *model* *is* *small (a *distilled* LLM, a *CNN) | *deployed* [I] |
| FPGA (Xilinx/AMD Alveo, Intel Agilex) | the *reconfigurable* data center | the *reconfigurability* *is the* *product: the *dataflow* *is* *changed* *in the* *field, *not in the* *fab* (the *FPGA* *is a* *"slow* *Groq"* — *the* *dataflow* *is* *scheduled, *but the* *fabric* *is* *reconfigurable at runtime) | *deployed* (niche in AI) [I] |

*The* *first-principles* *read:* **the* *NPU* *is the* *TSP/TPU thesis at the* *device* *scale* (the *SRAM* *regime, *the* *low* *watt), *and it* *is winning* *the* *edge* *inference* *market* (the *on-device* LLM *is a* *batch-1* *workload, *the* *exact* *regime* *the* *SRAM* *chip* *owns, page 17). *The* *DPU* *is a* *different* *question* *(the* *peripheral* offload, *not the* *matmul) — *it* *does not* *compete* *with the* *flagships; *it* *competes* *with the* *CPU's* *peripheral* *load. *The* *FPGA* *is the* *flagship thesis* *extended to a* *reconfigurable* *substrate: the* *dataflow* *is* *scheduled* (like *Groq), *but the* *fabric* *can be* *reprogrammed* (like a *FPGA, *not like a *fixed* *ASIC). *The* *trade-off* *is* *the* *reconfigurability* *costs* *the* *density* *and the* *determinism* (the *FPGA* *fabric* *is* *slower* *and* *less* *deterministic* *than* the *TSP's* *scheduled* *Dragonfly, *because* *the* *routing* *is* *reconfigurable, *not* *scheduled) [I].

## The reconfigurable and in-memory bets
| Direction | The bet | State | The first-principles question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconfigurable dataflow (e.g., *the* *Groq line extended; *FPGA-based; *the* *"dataflow* *ASIC"* *category) | the *dataflow* *is fixed* *at compile time, *but the* *fabric* *can be* *reconfigured* *between* *models (a *"soft" TSP) | *research / early* *product* [I] | *the* *reconfigurability* *vs the* *determinism* *trade: the* *TSP* *is* *deterministic* *because* *the* *fabric* *is* *fixed; the* *reconfigurable* *dataflow* *is* *flexible* *because* *the* *fabric* *is* *soft — *you* *cannot* *have* *both* [I] |
| Compute-in-memory (CIM, analog/digital) | the *matmul* *is done* *in the* *memory* *array* (the *bitlines* *are the* *adders), *eliminating the *data movement* *to a* *MAC unit* (page 03's *memory wall* *attacked at the* *bit level) | *research / early* *product* (the *analog* *CIM* *is* *in* *the* *NPU; *the* *digital* *CIM* *is* *in* *the* *research) | *the* *accuracy* *of* *the* *analog* *addition* (the *CIM* *is* *limited* *by* *the* *analog* *precision, *which is *why* *it is* *used* *for* *inference, *not* *training) [I] |
| Neuromorphic (Intel Loihi, IBM NorthPole) | the *spiking* *neuron* *is the* *compute unit; *the* *event-driven* *architecture* *is* *extremely* *low* *power* (the *neuron* *fires* *only* *when* *there is* *a* *spike), *but the* *precision* *is* *low (the *spike* *is a* *1-bit* *event) | *research / early* *product* | *the* *LLM* *needs* *high* *precision* (the *matmul* *is a* *32-bit* *accumulate); the* *neuromorphic* *architecture* *is* *low* *power* *but* *low* *precision — *a* *mismatch* *for* *LLM* *inference* [I] |
| Photonic (Lightmatter, *the* *photonic* *interconnect; *the* *optical* *matmul) | the *photonic* *fabric* *is the* *interconnect* (the *optical* *is* *faster* *and* *cooler* *than* *the* *electrical), *or the* *photonic* *matmul* (the *light* *is the* *MAC) | *research / early* *product* (Lightmatter's *passage* *is a* *photonic* *interconnect, *deployed as a* *chiplet-to-chiplet* link) | *the* *photonic* *matmul* *is* *still* *research; the* *photonic* *interconnect* *is* *deployed* (Lightmatter's *passage* *is* *in* *the* *NVIDIA/AMD* *chiplet* *designs, *UNVERIFIED* *on the* *exact* *integration) [F/I] |
| Analog (the *resistive* *matmul; the *memristor* *CIM) | the *analog* *precision* *is* *limited* (the *resistive* *is* *~4-6 bits), *but the* *energy* *is* *extremely* *low* | *research* | *the* *analog* *is* *the* *CIM line* *extended; the* *precision* *limit* *is* *the* *same* (page 20's *microscaling* *cannot* *fix* *the* *analog* *noise) [I] |

*The* *first-principles* *read:* **the* *reconfigurable/in-memory* *bets are* *all* *attacks on* *the same wall: the* *data movement.* *The* *TSP* *eliminates* *the data movement* *by* *scheduling* (page 14); *the* *reconfigurable dataflow* *eliminates* *it by* *reconfiguring; *the* *CIM* *eliminates* *it by* *computing in the* *memory; the* *neuromorphic* *eliminates* *it by* *event-driven* *computation; the* *photonic* *eliminates* *it by* *optical* *transport. *The* *flagships* *have won the* *rack* *with the* *SRAM/HBM* *regime; *the* *tail* *is* *competing* *for the* *device, *and for* the *next* *node's* *dataflow* [I].

*The* *first-principles* *read:* **the* *non-flagship* *accelerators* *are* *the* *flagships' theses, *extended to a* *different* *workload* *(Tesla: the* *on-vehicle; Meta: the* *in-house* *data center; Tenstorrent: the* *open* *position) *or a* *different* *substrate* *(Taalas: the* *wafer-scale; Etched: the* *dataflow; MatX: the* *inference* *ASIC). *The* *thesis* *is* *the* *same* (the *SRAM/HBM regime, the *scheduled* *dataflow, the *per-token* *cost); *the* *execution* *is* *different* (the *fab, *the* *workload, *the* *market). *The* *first-principles* *question* *for each: does the* *execution* *win the* *workload* *the* *flagship* *is* *in?* — *the* *Tesla* *Dojo* *has* *not* *won* *the* *training* *market* *(the* *NVIDIA* *CUDA* *moat* *is* *the* *reason, page 25); *the* *Meta* *MTIA* *is* *in* *the* *data center* *but* *not* *in the* *cloud; *the* *Tenstorrent* *is* *in* *the* *cloud* *and* *edge* *but* *not* *in* *the* *large* *data-center* *rack* [I].

## The "what if" questions (the tail vs. the flagships)
*The* *tail* *poses* *five* *questions to* *the* *flagships:
1. *NPU:* *can the* *on-device* LLM *beat the* *cloud* *for the* *batch-1* *workload?* — *yes, *for the* *small* *model* (a *7B* *distilled LLM* *fits* *in the* *phone's* *SRAM); *the* *NPU* *is the* *TSP thesis* *at the* *device* *scale* (page 17's *SRAM* *regime, *page 22's *batch-1* *mapping) [I].
2. *DPU:* *can the* *peripheral* *offload* *free the* *CPU* *for the* *AI workload?* — *yes, *for the* *network/storage* (the *DPU* *is* *the* *third* *processor, *not the* *fourth) [I].
3. *Reconfigurable dataflow:* *can the* *soft* *fabric* *match the* *hard* *fabric's* *determinism?* — *no, *not yet* (the *reconfigurability* *costs* *the* *determinism, page 16); *the* *reconfigurable* *dataflow* *is the* *middle* *ground* *between the* *FPGA* *and the* *ASIC, *and it* *is* *a* *real* *product* *category* (the *FPGA-based* *AI* *accelerators, *the* *reconfigurable* *ASIC) [I].
4. *CIM / neuromorphic / photonic:* *can the* *in-memory* *or the* *optical* *beat the* *SRAM/HBM regime?* — *the* *CIM* *is* *in* *the* *NPU* (the *analog* *matmul* *is* *the* *phone's* *matmul); *the* *photonic* *is* *in* *the* *interconnect* (Lightmatter's *passage); *the* *neuromorphic* *is* *in* *the* *research* (the *spiking* *is* *low* *power* *but* *low* *precision) [I].
5. *The non-flagship:* *can the* *in-house* *chip* *beat the* *flagship* *for the* *in-house* *workload?* — *the* *Meta* *MTIA* *and* *the* *Tesla* *Dojo* *are* *the* *answers: the* *in-house* *chip* *is* *optimized* *for* *the* *in-house* *workload, *and it* *is* *cheaper* *at* *scale* (the *per-token* *cost* *is* *lower, *because* *the* *chip* *is* *not* *a* *product, *it is* *a* *component) [I].
