## The confirmed contradictions (surfaced, not buried)
*Three* *claims* *in* *the* *anchor* *do* *not* *survive* *the* *primary-source* *check. *None* *is* *trivial; *each* *is* *the* *kind* *of* *error* *a* *seed* *article* *makes* *when* *it* *is* *a* *narrative, *not a* *citation:
1. **BERT-Large latency ~75 µs** *(the* *biggest). *The* *anchor* *says* *"24,240 runs of BERT-Large returned inside a ~75 µs band, and the compiler's predicted latency sat within 2% of measured."* *The* *ISCA* *2022* *paper* *(§5.4)* *describes* *the* *identical* *experiment* *(4 TSPs, *24,240 runs, *SQuAD1.1 dev, *input read/write over PCIe included) *and* *reports* **P99 < 1,225 µs / P100 = 1,300 µs** — *a* *~16×* *gap* *from* *the* *anchor's* *number. *The* *most* *likely* *explanation* *[I]:* *the* *anchor* *reads* *Figure* *17's* *histogram* *at* *the* *bin* *resolution* *(5 µs bins, *the* *x-axis* *is* *zoomed)* *as* *an* *absolute* *value, *or* *cites a* *different* *Groq* *internal* *measurement* *not* *in* *the* *paper.* *This* *section* *uses* *the* *ISCA* *number* *(page* *14) *and* *never* *the* *anchor's* *75 µs.*
2. **The deal value ~$13B vs ~$20B.** *The* *anchor* *says* *"roughly* *$13B* *paid* *at* *closing, *which* *led* *the* *press* *to* *call* *it* *one* *(an* *acquisition).*"* *The* *NVIDIA* *press* *release* *and* *CNBC* *(2025-12-24)* *both* *put* *the* *figure* *at* *~$20B; *the* *precise* *number* *circulating* *in* *the* *10-K* *coverage* *is* *~$20.6B* *[F: press]. *The* *anchor's* *$13B* *may* *be a* *different* *accounting* *(license* *fee* *vs* *total* *consideration), *but* *it* *is* *not* *the* *figure* *this* *section* *can* *verify* *against* *a* *primary* *source.* *We* *use* *~$20.6B.*
3. **The TPU v1 attribution.** *The* *anchor's* *opening* *attributes* *the* *29×/80×* *TPU* *v1* *figures* *to a* *"Turing* *Lecture"* *by* *Hennessy* *&* *Patterson* *at* *ISCA* *2018.* *Those* *two* *numbers* *are* *indeed* *from* *the* *Lecture, *not* *from* *the* *TPU* *v1* *paper* *(arXiv:1704.04760), *which* *states* *the* *92 TOPS* *INT8* *and* *28 MiB* *SRAM* *but* *not* *the* *29×/80×* *CPU* *ratios.* *The* *Lecture* *is* *the* *correct* *source* *for* *the* *ratios; *the* *paper* *is* *the* *correct* *source* *for* *the* *absolute* *figures. *This* *section* *cites* *both* *separately* *(page* *10).

*The* *first-principles* *lesson:* **the anchor is a narrative, not a citation.** *Its* *numbers* *are* *usually* *right* *in* *order* *of* *magnitude* *(which* *is* *why* *it* *serves* *as a* *seed), *but* *the* *exact* *figures, *the* *unit* *labels, *and the* *deal* *terms* *are* *where* *narratives* *drift* *from* *the* *primary* *record. *The* *audit* *is* *the* *procedure* *that* *catches* *the* *drift: *every* *number* *in* *this* *section* *is* *either* *[F]* *with* *a* *primary* *source, *[E]* *computed* *from* *[F]* *data, *[I]* *inferred, *[A]* *assumed, *or* *UNVERIFIED — *and* *the* *anchor* *is* *never* *the* *last* *citation [I].
